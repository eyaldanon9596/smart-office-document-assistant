INTERNAL REPORT -- Q3 Facilities Usage Summary

Prepared by: Facilities Team
Date: 15 September 2026

This report summarizes meeting room utilization for Q3 2026. Overall
occupancy averaged 62%, up from 54% in Q2. The 4th floor conference rooms
saw the highest demand, particularly Tuesday and Wednesday afternoons.

No changes to booking policy are recommended at this time. This report is
for informational purposes only.
